# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Shift pitch by up to 12 semitones up or down, with fine tuning to 0.01 of a semitone.
- Change tempo from 25% to 250% without changing pitch.
- Loop between two points, with a speed ramp that raises the tempo on each pass.
- Split a song into vocals, drums, bass, and other, on your computer. Mute any stem while it plays.
- Record tab audio for stem separation on sites that don't expose an audio file.
- Keyboard shortcuts in the popup, and global shortcuts for pitch and tempo.
- Remembers pitch, tuning, and tempo for each page.
- Light and dark themes.
